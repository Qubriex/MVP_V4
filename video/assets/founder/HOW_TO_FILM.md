# Founder footage: how to film (phone is fine)

Three clips for the investor video. Name them exactly as below and put them in this folder.

| File | Length | What to say (from scripts/en/investor-full.md) |
|---|---|---|
| intro.mp4 | 20-25 s | Scene 1: who you are + the problem |
| traction.mp4 | 20-25 s | Scene 9: traction + team |
| ask.mp4 | 15-20 s | Scene 10: the ask + thank you |

- **Camera:** phone on a stand at eye level, landscape, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps, back camera.
- **Light:** face a window; no bright window behind you.
- **Sound:** lapel mic if possible; quiet room; no fan.
- **Frame:** head and shoulders, eyes in the top third, plain background.
- **Delivery:** speak to the lens, not the screen. Do 3 takes of each. Pause 1 s before and after.
- **Clothes:** plain, no small stripes or logos.
